2022The space

A new home in Dwarka, with the whole ground floor kept aside for a studio.

NowFrom the studio to the spotlight

Big Web Studios is back as a premium studio and agency. The podcast and content space is open, the music studio is on the way, and we help musicians, creators and brands go from the first take to the stage.
