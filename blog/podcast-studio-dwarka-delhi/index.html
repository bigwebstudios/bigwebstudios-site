Podcast studio in Dwarka, New Delhi: rates, gear and how booking works

Big Web Studios · 8 November 2026

Short answer: Big Web Studios runs a podcast studio opposite Dwarka Sector 19 in New Delhi. It costs ₹2,000 per hour for up to 6 people, with 10% off the studio rate when you book 4 hours or more. It's open every day from 10:00 to 22:00 and you can book by the hour online.

What you get in the room

The podcast studio is the large room on our ground floor, roughly 13'6" × 19'. It's set up for video podcasts, not just audio, so the light and backdrops are already in place when you walk in.

  • 2 podcast mics, set up and ready
  • A main light and 2 RGB fill lights
  • White and grey backdrops, plus a green screen
  • 2 tripods for your cameras or phones
  • Two sets in the room, with space for multi-camera shots
  • Its own walkway, so nobody crosses your frame mid-take

You can book the room on its own and run the session yourself, or add a sound engineer to set levels and record for you. The camera operator add-on is one operator; you bring or rent the cameras.

How booking works

  1. Open the booking page and choose Podcast studio.
  2. Tap the hours you want on the timeline, or drag across a day to pick a block.
  3. Set how many people are coming and any add-ons. The session sheet shows your estimate as you go.
  4. Add your name, WhatsApp number and email, then tap Book this session.

You'll get an email with the booking details, the studio rules and a calendar invite. Nothing is charged online. You pay at the studio or by UPI.
