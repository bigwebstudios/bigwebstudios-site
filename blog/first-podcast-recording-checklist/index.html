Your first podcast recording: a simple checklist

Big Web Studios · 8 November 2026

Short answer: Before your first studio podcast recording, write an outline, confirm your guests, decide the format (audio or video) and book enough time. On the day, arrive a few minutes early, silence phones, do a short test recording and keep water nearby.

A week before

  • Write an outline. Not a script, just the main points and questions in order.
  • Confirm your guests and send them the address, time and what to wear on camera.
  • Decide the format. Audio only, or video too? Video needs outfits, framing and someone watching the cameras.
  • Book enough time. For a 45–60 minute episode, book 2 hours.

The day before

  • Charge every camera, phone and battery.
  • Clear space on memory cards and phones.
  • Print or save the outline so everyone can see it.
  • Pick plain clothes. Small stripes and checks can flicker on camera.

On the day

  • Arrive a few minutes early so setup doesn't eat into your session.
  • Put every phone on silent, including guests'.
  • Record 30 seconds, then listen back on headphones before the real take.
  • Keep water nearby. Avoid fizzy drinks right before talking.
  • Leave a few seconds of silence at the start and end. It makes editing easier.

Questions people ask

Do I need a script for a podcast?

No. An outline with your main points and questions in order works better. It keeps the conversation natural.

What should I wear for a video podcast?

Plain, solid colours. Small stripes and checks can flicker on camera.

How early should I arrive?

A few minutes before your booked time, so your session starts on time.

How long does setup take?

Leave about 15–20 minutes of your booking for setup and a test recording.
