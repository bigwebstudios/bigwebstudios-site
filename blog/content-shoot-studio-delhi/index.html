Content shoot studio in Delhi for reels and product shoots: what to bring

Big Web Studios · 8 November 2026

Short answer: Big Web Studios rents its room for content shoots at ₹999 per hour for up to 4 people, near Dwarka Sector 19 in New Delhi. Lights, white and grey backdrops, a green screen and 2 tripods are included. Bring your camera or phone, outfits, products and a shot list.

What's in the room

Content shoots use the same room as the podcast studio, with the floor opened up for movement and product setups.

  • A main light and 2 RGB fill lights
  • White and grey backdrops
  • A green screen
  • 2 tripods

It's ₹999 per hour for up to 4 people, and 10% off the studio rate if you book 3 hours or more. You can add a camera operator for ₹1,000 per hour. Editing is quoted per project.

What to bring

  • Your camera or phone, fully charged, with plenty of free storage.
  • Outfits on hangers, so they don't crease on the way.
  • Products and props, cleaned and ready, with spares if they can get damaged on camera.
  • A shot list. Write down every reel, angle and product shot you need, in order.
  • Reference reels saved on your phone, so everyone on set knows the look you want.

Plan your hours

Group shots by backdrop and lighting. Do all the white-backdrop shots together, then switch. Changing the set takes time, so fewer switches means more content per hour.
