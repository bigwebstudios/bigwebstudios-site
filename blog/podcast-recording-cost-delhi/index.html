How much does it cost to record a podcast in Delhi?

Big Web Studios · 8 November 2026

Short answer: At Big Web Studios, a typical one-hour podcast episode costs about ₹4,000 for the room (2 hours booked), or about ₹5,600 with a sound engineer. Longer sessions of 4 hours or more get 10% off the studio rate. Editing is quoted separately.

The three things you pay for

A podcast recording usually has three costs: the room, the people who help you run it, and the edit afterwards. You can skip the last two if you're comfortable doing them yourself.

  1. The studio. ₹2,000 per hour for up to 6 people, with 10% off the studio rate for 4+ hours.
  2. Help on the day. A sound engineer is +₹800 per hour. A camera operator is +₹1,000 per hour.
  3. Editing. Episode edits and short clips are quoted per project, because it depends on length, cameras and how many clips you want.

How many hours to book

Book more time than the length of the episode. A good rule is to plan for setup, a few false starts and a short break.

  • One 45–60 minute episode: book 2 hours.
  • Two episodes back to back: book 3 to 4 hours.
  • A batch day (3–4 episodes): book 5 to 6 hours and take the 4+ hour discount.

Ways to keep the cost down

  • Record several episodes in one longer session to get the 4+ hour discount.
  • Prepare your outline and guest list before the day, so studio time goes on recording.
  • Run the session yourself if someone on your team is comfortable with audio levels.

Questions people ask

What is the cheapest way to record a podcast in a studio?

Book the room on its own and run the session yourself. At Big Web Studios that's ₹2,000 per hour, and 10% less on the studio rate if you book 4 hours or more.

How long should I book for one episode?

For a 45–60 minute episode, book 2 hours. That leaves time for setup, a few retakes and a break.

Is editing included?

No. Editing is quoted per project, depending on length, number of cameras and how many short clips you need.

Do I pay online?

No. Nothing is charged online. You pay at the studio or by UPI.
